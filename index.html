代表收尾 · 约 30 秒Closing · About 30 seconds

我们并不否认人类会从奖励中学习。我们的反对意见是：这种相似性，不足以证明简单 RL 适合作为人类决策的通用解释。奖励推断存在歧义，记忆与任务理解影响选择，探索包含信息目标，而相似行为也可能来自不同机制。因此，Group 1 需要提供能够区分替代解释、预测新行为的证据，才能证明其模型适合所讨论的人类决策。

We do not deny that humans learn from rewards. Our objection is that this similarity does not establish simple RL as a general explanation of human decision-making. Rewards are ambiguous, memory and task understanding matter, exploration can seek information, and different mechanisms can produce similar behaviour. Group 1 therefore needs evidence that distinguishes competing explanations and predicts new human choices.

现场引用，守住这三条。Three boundaries for the debate.

  • 不说“RL 没有记忆”。循环 RL 可以整合历史信息。Do not say RL has no memory. Recurrent RL can integrate history.
  • 不说“RL 只能随机探索”。我们的批评针对单纯 ε-greedy。Do not say RL only explores randomly. Our critique targets epsilon-greedy alone.
  • 60.6% 与 68.3% 是特定研究的行为预测准确率，不是所有 RL 的能力上限。60.6% and 68.3% are results from one study, not universal performance limits.
